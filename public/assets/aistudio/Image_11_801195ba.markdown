## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, retro-futuristic fintech aesthetic tailored for Indonesian credit unions (*koperasi simpan pinjam*) and MSMEs (*UMKM*). The design balances institutional credibility, communal warmth, and precision fraud surveillance. It trades sterile corporate blues for organic warmth and jade luminescence, evoking trust, vitality, and advanced pattern detection.

### Tone & Personality
- **Guardian & Vigilant:** Analytical precision, real-time threat detection, and clarity under stress.
- **Warm Institutional Trust:** Anchored in Indonesian cooperative heritage (*gotong royong* and *kekeluargaan*) through warm peach and linen undertones.
- **Tactile Retro-Futurism:** Late-70s control-room ergonomics updated with crisp modern vector clarity, phosphor glows, and technical monospaced telemetry.

### Visual Principles
- **Luminous Glass over Warm Paper:** Frosted white panels floating on a gradient background (#FFF8F4 to #FFE9DF), paired with structural borders.
- **Instrument-Grade Information Hierarchy:** Immediate differentiation between standard operational flow and flagged anomalies using high-contrast semantic signaling.
- **Local Context First:** Indonesian language terminology structured for administrators, treasurers (*bendahara*), and audit officers.

## Colors

The palette relies on a warm atmospheric base paired with jade-green telemetry, retro-alarm reds, and cautionary yellows.

### Color Tokens & Rules
- **Base Canvas:** Linear gradient from `#FFF8F4` (top) to `#FFE9DF` (bottom). Never render flat cold whites as canvas backgrounds.
- **Primary Brand (Jade Green):** `#009770` serves as the primary visual anchor for active states, key data paths, primary controls, and verified badges.
- **Bright Jade Glow:** `#00B386` is used exclusively for gradient endings (`#009770` to `#00B386`), hover glows, and live telemetry pulses.
- **Light Jade Tint:** `#E0F4EE` provides the background for active navigation nodes, icon badges, and verified status containers.
- **High Risk / Danger Alert:** Retro Red `#CE2F2E`. Strictly reserved for critical fraud indicators, high-risk flags, anomaly scores exceeding thresholds, and destructive confirmations.
- **Warning / Review Flag:** Vanilla `#FFF637`. Always rendered with high-contrast olive-gold text `#6B5F00`. Yellow text on white or light backgrounds is prohibited.
- **Soft Peach:** `#FFB89F` drives secondary visual interest: radar scan lines, decorative micro dot grids, and low-priority container accents.
- **Typography & Surfaces:**
  - Primary Content / Headers: Deep Jade-Black `#0A2A22`.
  - Secondary / Supporting: Muted Bark `#6B5A55`.
  - Glass Card Background: Pure White `#FFFFFF` with glassmorphic transparency (92% opacity) and 1px border `#F3D9CE`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between human reading comfort and scientific measurement:

1. **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Provides structured geometric energy with subtle retro-futuristic nuances in letterforms (e.g., counters and terminals). Used for screen titles, metrics, and KPI readouts.
2. **Body & Interface (Inter):** Maximizes interface legibility across tabular transaction feeds, contextual explanations, and Indonesian administrative workflows.
3. **Data Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Applied to monetary values (`Rp`), transaction IDs, timestamps, anomaly thresholds, and national identity formats (NIK, NIB). Tabular numbers ensure vertical alignment across data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The dashboard uses a desktop-first shell designed for standard office displays (1440px target, min 1280px, responsive down to 1024px tablet landscape).

### Desktop Shell Structure
- **Persistent Slim Sidebar:** Fixed width of 240px. Hosts branding, identity, and navigation.
- **Top Utility Header:** Fixed height of 64px spanning across the content canvas.
- **Content Viewport:** 12-column responsive grid with 1.5rem (24px) gutters and 2rem (32px) screen margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Density
- Component interiors utilize an 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` 8px, `space-md` 16px, `space-lg` 24px).
- Generous padding inside cards gives transaction lists and risk heatmaps clean breathing room against high-density data.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass strata, warm contact shadows, and low-contrast borders rather than deep, cold dropshadows.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Level 0 (Atmosphere):** Gradient canvas (`#FFF8F4` to `#FFE9DF`) overlayed with faint decorative dot grids (`#FFB89F` at 25% opacity, 16px pitch).
- **Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` with 92% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Enclosed by a 1px solid border `#F3D9CE`. Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(107, 90, 85, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(107, 90, 85, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Tooltips & Threat Drawers):** High-opacity pure white (98%), border `#F3D9CE`, shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 42, 34, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 42, 34, 0.06)`.
- **Live Signal Layers (Radar Pulses):** Glowing highlights using `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 179, 134, 0.45)` for Jade and `0 0 12px rgba(206, 47, 46, 0.4)` for Retro Red alerts.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, rounded geometries (roundedness tier `2`) providing 16px (`1rem`) to 20px corners on main functional containers, avoiding sharp industrial brutality while retaining control-panel discipline.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Dashboard Metric Cards & Tables:** `1rem` (16px) border-radius.
- **Modal Viewports & Threat Drawers:** `1.25rem` (20px) border-radius.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** `0.75rem` (12px) border-radius.
- **Badges & Status Tags:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`).
- **Icons & Micro-Indicators:** 8px to 10px rounded squares for icon background badges.

## Components

### 1. Left Navigation Sidebar
- **Dimensions & Background:** Fixed width 240px, full viewport height, background translucent `#FFFFFF` (85% opacity, backdrop blur 16px), right border 1px solid `#F3D9CE`.
- **Logo Area:** Bold Space Grotesk typemark **AmanKas** in Deep Jade `#0A2A22`, accompanied by a Jade Green geometric shield/vault icon.
- **Navigation Links:** Height 44px, horizontal padding 12px, border-radius 12px.
  - *Default:* Text `#6B5A55`, icon clean 1.5px outline stroke `#6B5A55`.
  - *Active:* Background `#E0F4EE`, text `#009770` (font-weight 600), icon `#009770`, left-edge vertical indicator pill (3px width, `#009770`).
- **Live System Health:** Bottom module featuring a pulsing green radar dot labeled `RADAR AKTIF` in JetBrains Mono.

### 2. Header Bar
- **Search Element:** Pill-shaped input container, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#F3D9CE`, search icon in `#6B5A55`, placeholder `Cari NIK, ID Transaksi, atau Nama Anggota...`.
- **Notification Trigger:** 40px circular glass button. Bell icon with an active Retro Red `#CE2F2E` 8px badge with subtle radar pulse.
- **User Profile Pill:** Enclosed capsule containing avatar, and label `Bendahara · Sari W.` with metadata in JetBrains Mono sm.

### 3. Metric & Fraud Anomaly Cards
- **Card Chassis:** White glassmorphic surface, 1px border `#F3D9CE`, 20px padding.
- **Headline Layout:** Top row displays technical label (Inter sm, uppercase, tracking +0.05em, text `#6B5A55`) plus icon container (32x32px, `#E0F4EE` background, jade icon).
- **Metric Presentation:** Large numerical metric in Space Grotesk or JetBrains Mono (`Rp 1.420.000.000` or `0.04%`).
- **Trend Footers:** Contextual delta capsules displaying "+12% dari minggu lalu" in jade or retro red.

### 4. Status Badges & Pill Tags

  - Text: `#009770`
  - Indicator: 6px solid circle `#009770`
- **Perlu Ditinjau (Warning):**
  - Background: `#FFF637`
  - Text: `#6B5F00`
  - Indicator: 6px solid circle `#6B5F00`

  - Background: `#FDE8E8`
  - Text: `#CE2F2E`

### 5. Buttons & Controls
- **Primary Action:** Linear gradient background from `#009770` to `#00B386`, text `#FFFFFF` (font-weight 600), subtle inner glow, shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0, 151, 112, 0.25)`. Active scale 0.98.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent glass background, 1.5px border `#009770`, text `#009770`. Hover background `#E0F4EE`.
- **Danger Action:** Solid Retro Red `#CE2F2E` background, white bold text. Used exclusively for fraud quarantines (`Bekukan Akun`, `Tolak Transaksi`).

### 6. Data Tables (Audit Stream)
- **Header Cells:** Background `#FFF8F4`, text `#6B5A55`, JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase, border-bottom 1px solid `#F3D9CE`.
- **Rows:** Zebra alternating between `#FFFFFF` and `rgba(255, 248, 244, 0.4)`. Hover state triggers soft jade-tinted row `#F3FAF7`.
- **Data Formatting:** Monetary values and timestamps right-aligned in tabular JetBrains Mono figures.
